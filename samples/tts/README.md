# TTS listening sample files

These are generated outputs selected by case ID, plus FLEURS and LibriTTS voice
references needed to interpret the cloning examples. No listener scores or
preferences are collected in this directory.

See [`docs/tts-listening.md`](../../docs/tts-listening.md) for prompts and
links. Model revisions and output hashes are recorded under `results/tts/`.

The references are CC BY 4.0. The Romanian recording comes from the FLEURS
`ro_ro` test split published by Google through Hugging Face. The English
recordings come from the LibriTTS `test-clean` split published through OpenSLR.
Generated outputs keep any restrictions imposed by their model licences; see
`docs/tts-models.md`.
